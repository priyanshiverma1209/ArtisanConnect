---
name: Role-first experience
description: Durable product decision for ArtisanConnect authentication and role-specific navigation.
---

The app should ask whether someone is a customer or artisan before account creation, then keep the two experiences separate after sign-in. Customer onboarding stays short; artisan onboarding collects enough craft and identity context for later verification.

**Why:** The target audience includes people with limited literacy or technical experience, and a shared dashboard with a role switch was confusing.

**How to apply:** Preserve plain labels, one clear next action, role-specific navigation, and separate home pages when adding persistence, messaging, verification, or mobile support.
